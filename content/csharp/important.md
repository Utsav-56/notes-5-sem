
Based on a detailed analysis of the past Tribhuvan University (Faculty of Humanities and Social Science) exam papers for **Dot Net Technology** (Batch years 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, and 2021), here are the most repeated questions and topics sorted by frequency.

### **Most Repeated Questions by Frequency**

**5 times (Appeared in Every Paper):**
1.  **LINQ Queries & Operators:** Write a program using LINQ (to select, sort, or filter data like salary, student records, or odd numbers). (2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021)
2.  **Operator Overloading:** Define or write a program to overload binary (+, -), relational (==, <), or unary (++) operators. (2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021)
3.  **Exception Handling:** Explain exception handling keywords or write a program to handle specific exceptions like `DivideByZero`, `IndexOutOfRange`, or `Custom Exceptions`. (2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021)
4.  **ASP.NET Web Forms:** Create an ASP.NET web page for user registration, employee management, or simple interest calculation and display data. (2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021)

**4 times:**
1.  **ADO.NET & Database Connectivity:** Explain differences between `ExecuteReader`, `ExecuteScalar`, and `ExecuteNonQuery` or `DataAdapter` vs `DataReader`. (2017, 2018, 2019, 2021)
2.  **Delegates and Lambda Expressions:** Define delegates, generic delegates, or explain lambda expressions with suitable programs. (2017, 2019, 2020, 2021)

**3 times:**
1.  **Value Type vs Reference Type:** Explain differences with concepts like boxing and unboxing. (2018, 2019, 2020)
2.  **Virtual Methods & Dynamic Binding:** How are virtual methods used to achieve dynamic polymorphism/binding? (2017, 2019, 2021)
3.  **Interfaces & Multiple Inheritance:** How can interfaces be used to perform multiple inheritance in C#? (2017, 2018, 2020)
4.  **Indexers:** Difference between indexer and properties or how to declare an indexer. (2019, 2020, 2021)
5.  **Generics:** Define generics and explain different types of generic classes. (2017, 2019, 2021)
6.  **Constructors & Base Keyword:** Explain types of constructors (static, copy) and use of `base` keyword to call parent constructors. (2017, 2019, 2020)

---

### **Mostly Repeated Topics (Appearing >3 times)**

**1. LINQ (Language Integrated Query)**
*   **Frequency:** Every single year.
*   **Marks Approx:** Always carries **5 to 10 marks**. Usually appears as a programming question in Group B or Group C.

**2. Operator Overloading**
*   **Frequency:** Every single year.
*   **Marks Approx:** Usually **5 to 10 marks**. It is a staple of Group C (Long Questions).

**3. Exception Handling**
*   **Frequency:** Every single year.
*   **Marks Approx:** **5 to 10 marks**. Often combined with programs to demonstrate `try-catch-finally` or custom exceptions.

**4. ASP.NET Web Forms & Server Controls**
*   **Frequency:** Every single year.
*   **Marks Approx:** **10 marks**. Almost always the final or second-to-last question in Group C, requiring a UI design and logic.

**5. Database Connectivity (ADO.NET)**
*   **Frequency:** 4 out of 5 years.
*   **Marks Approx:** **5 to 10 marks**. Focuses heavily on the different "Execute" methods and the architecture of DataReaders/Adapters.

**6. Delegates and Lambda Expressions**
*   **Frequency:** 4 out of 5 years.
*   **Marks Approx:** **5 marks**. Frequently appears in Group B as a conceptual question or a short program.

**7. Inheritance and Interfaces**
*   **Frequency:** 4 out of 5 years (when including general inheritance and interface specific questions).
*   **Marks Approx:** **5 marks**. Usually focuses on the "Multiple Inheritance" limitation of C# and how Interfaces solve it.
